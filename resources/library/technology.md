# Technology and Infrastructure (TI) field guide

Technology and infrastructure covers the devices, internet, shared tools and data systems an organization needs to work well and keep information safe. Among the 2025 organizations with the lowest overall scores, technology was the biggest growth area (2025 OCI cohort findings). The aim is not fancy software; it is reliable, secure tools that make frontline work easier.

## By stage

### Seeding and Emerging: reliable basics

- Make sure every staff member has a working device, internet access and a phone plan that fits field work (OCI questionnaire, Technology and Infrastructure).
- Use one shared set of tools for email, calendars and files, so work does not live on personal phones (OCI questionnaire, Technology and Infrastructure).
- Turn on multi-factor authentication, use strong unique passwords and keep devices updated. These steps block many common attacks on small teams (CISA, Cyber Guidance for Small Businesses).
- Store participant information in a secure, access-controlled place, not in personal notes or open spreadsheets.

### Developing: systems that support the work

- Use a simple case management or tracking system for participant and program data, and train staff to use it (OCI questionnaire, Technology and Infrastructure).
- Put data systems in place before expanding services. Programs that launched without them faced unclear records that were hard to fix years later (HVIP focus group brief, 2026).
- Write down how you protect sensitive data and confidentiality, including who can see what (OCI questionnaire, Technology and Infrastructure).
- Choose tools that fit frontline workflows. Systems should produce reliable information without adding burden for staff whose strength is relationships (HVIP focus group brief, 2026).

### Strengthening and Transformational: integrated and resilient

- Connect program, finance and fundraising data so reports are built from one source (OCI questionnaire, Technology and Infrastructure).
- Give someone dedicated time, on staff or as a consultant, to manage systems and review them each year.
- Use data to support analysis, reporting and decisions (OCI questionnaire, Technology and Infrastructure).
- Make a continuity plan for outages and emergencies, including backups and how staff will reach each other (OCI questionnaire, Technology and Infrastructure).
- Make sure partners also have the tools, training and technology they need to track shared work (BJA CVI Implementation Checklist, Step 5.1).

## Practical tips

- Budget for technology as part of program costs, including data systems and training (HVIP focus group brief, 2026).
- Start with backups. Losing case records can harm participants and the organization.
- Treat confidentiality as trust. Participants share sensitive details; protecting them is part of trauma-informed care.

## Team conversation starters

- If a staff phone were lost today, what participant information would be exposed?
- Which daily task wastes the most time because of our tools?
- Who would keep us running during an outage?
